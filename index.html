Hafele Gas Stove Repair and Maintenance

Hafele gas stoves and built-in hobs may develop issues like low flame, yellow flame, one side burner not igniting, auto-ignition failure, gas leakage smell, knob jam or burner not staying on. These problems can arise due to blocked burner jets, faulty spark plug, damaged ignition wiring, worn-out knobs, dust in gas pipe or improper installation. Our independent technician in Ghaziabad inspects the hob model, checks burner assembly, tests ignition continuity, inspects gas inlet and outlet, examines knob operation and explains repair options clearly before work. We focus on safe diagnosis, compatible component replacement where available and proper fitting to ensure stable flame and safe operation. Do not attempt to open gas line without technical knowledge. If you smell strong gas, close supply and ventilate area. We provide transparent estimate, no hidden charges and practical maintenance tips for long-term hob care. Our aim is to provide safe, practical and suitable repair solutions for Hafele gas hobs and stoves in residential kitchens.
