How to Prepare for a Chimney or Hob Service Enquiry

A few details can help clarify the issue before a visit is discussed.

1. Identify the Appliance

If possible, note the model number from the product label, manual or purchase documentation. The model can help identify the appliance configuration and the relevant maintenance instructions.

2. Describe the Symptoms

For a chimney, mention whether the lights, controls, fan and suction behave normally, and when the sound or airflow problem began. For a hob, describe the control or ignition symptom without opening the appliance or attempting internal repairs.

3. Explain the Kitchen Setup

Installation and airflow questions may depend on the mounting height, duct route, outlet position and available space. Photos of the setup can be useful when arranging an assessment, but avoid sharing sensitive personal information in images.

4. Confirm Charges and Parts Before Work

Ask about inspection or visit charges, estimated repair costs, parts availability and expected timing before approving work. Any applicable parts warranty should be explained clearly in writing.
