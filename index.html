Common Chimney & Hob Problems

Choose the issue that most closely matches what you are experiencing and explain it in your enquiry.

Weak chimney suction Possible causes include dirty filters, duct restrictions or a component problem.
Unusual noise or vibration Noise may be related to installation, loose parts, airflow or motor components.
Grease or filter build-up Cleaning needs depend on cooking frequency, filter design and appliance instructions.
Hob ignition or burner concern Describe the hob type and symptoms. If you smell gas, turn off the supply if safe, ventilate the area and do not operate electrical switches; contact your gas provider or emergency service.
