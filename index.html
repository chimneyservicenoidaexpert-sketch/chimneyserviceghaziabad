🧹 Hafele Chimney Deep Cleaning Service - Full Description

Our Hafele Chimney Deep Cleaning is not just wiping from outside. We do complete dismantling and chemical wash of all greasy parts. This is most demanded service in Ghaziabad because Indian cooking produces heavy oil smoke.

  • Step 1 - Inspection: Technician checks chimney model, suction power, noise level, oil leakage, and grease level.
  • Step 2 - Safe Dismantling: Power off, remove filters, oil collector, blower fan cover carefully without damaging PCB or motor.
  • Step 3 - Chemical Degreasing: Soak filters, trays, fan blades in industrial-grade degreaser for 15-20 mins to melt sticky oil. Brush cleaning of motor housing and inner body.
  • Step 4 - High-Pressure Wash & Steam: Wash all parts with water jet and steam to remove 99% oil. Dry completely to avoid rust.
  • Step 5 - Duct & Pipe Cleaning: Clean external duct pipe which is main reason for low suction. Remove bird nests, dust, oil blockage.
  • Step 6 - Reassembly & Testing: Apply oil-repellent coating on filters, reassemble, oil moving parts, test suction, noise, lights, auto-clean button.

Benefits: 2x suction boost, zero oil dripping, noise 70% less, no bad smell, motor life increases by 2 years, kitchen tiles remain clean, electricity consumption less.

When to do deep cleaning? Every 4-6 months if daily non-veg/frying, every 8 months if normal veg cooking. If you see oil dripping or hear heavy sound, book immediately.

🔧 Hafele Chimney Repair Services - Detailed

⚙️ Motor Repair & Replacement

Motor is heart of chimney. If motor is humming but not rotating, rotating slowly, overheating, or burning smell, motor needs service. Common in Kallisto, Leo, Datura models after 2-3 years. We open motor, clean carbon dust, check bearing, lubricate shaft, test capacitor, check winding. If winding burnt, we suggest compatible original-grade motor with 90 days warranty. We don't replace motor directly without checking PCB and capacitor, saving your money.

💨 Low Suction Problem - Main Reason Customers Call Us

Your chimney is running but smoke goes in room, not in chimney. 90% cases: filters choked, duct pipe blocked, blower fan full of oil, or wrong installation height. We check step-by-step: First clean filters, then check blower fan rotation, then check duct pipe outlet on terrace (often blocked by polythene or birds), then check motor speed, then check if chimney installed too high from gas stove (should be 24-30 inch). After fixing, suction becomes double. We also suggest correct ducting (6 inch rigid pipe, minimum bends) for best performance.

🔊 Noise & Vibration Repair

Hafele chimney making khar-khar, ghar-ghar, or vibration sound? Reason: Loose screws, blower touching body due to oil weight, bearing dry, motor shaft bend, or loose wall mounting. In filter-free models like Tiara, Ivaraa, small screw loosens and touches fan. We tighten full body, balance fan, lubricate bearing, put rubber padding on mounting brackets, reduce noise to almost silent. If bearing damaged, we replace with German bearing for long life.

⚡ Chimney Not Starting - PCB & Touch Issue

Press button but no light, no sound? Or touch panel not responding? Or gesture not working in Kallisto models? Could be power supply fault, fuse burnt, PCB carbon due to oil, touch sensor dead, wiring cut by rats. We test with multimeter: Input supply, fuse, PCB output to motor, touch continuity. We clean PCB with contact cleaner, resolder dry joints, replace touch sensor if needed. For gesture models, we clean gesture sensor glass which gets oily. 80% cases repaired without replacing full PCB, saving Rs 2000-3000.

💧 Oil Leakage & Dripping Fix

Oil dripping from chimney sides on gas stove? This is very common. Oil collector tray full, drain pipe blocked, filters saturated, chimney installed tilted. In Datura and Leo models, anti-drip pipe gets blocked with solidified oil. We empty and wash oil collector, clear pipe with thin wire and hot water, level chimney with spirit level, and put extra oil-repellent tape on joints. Problem solved permanently if deep cleaning done every 6 months.

🏠 Hafele Chimney Installation Service - Correct Method

Wrong installation is reason for 50% complaints. Chimney should be 24-30 inch above hob, perfectly horizontal, duct pipe minimum 6 inch diameter, minimum bends, outlet facing away from wind, with proper wall support to bear 15-20kg weight. Many carpenters install with 3 inch flexible aluminum pipe with 4 bends - suction zero. We install with rigid PVC or SS duct, seal all joints with aluminum tape, put bird net on outlet, test with smoke paper, check auto-clean and lights. We also install for island kitchens and do reinstallation if you shift house. We explain you daily maintenance: How to use auto-clean weekly, how to clean oil tray monthly.

🔄 Hafele Chimney Maintenance Tips - Free Advice

  • Press auto-clean button once every week, put small bowl with water + degreaser below, let cycle complete 10 mins.
  • Remove oil collector tray every 15 days, wash with hot water + dish soap, dry and refit.
  • Wipe touch panel daily with dry cloth, not wet - water damages touch.
  • Clean outer body with Colin, not with hard scrubber which scratches black glass.
  • If cooking heavy frying, run chimney 5 mins after cooking also to suck remaining smoke.
  • Never pour water directly inside chimney motor area.
  • Keep model sticker photo in phone - helps us bring correct parts.
